Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of meetha bharat sweets?

Our dry fruit and cashew varieties (like Kaju Katli, Anjeer Roll, and Dry Fruit Katli) have a shelf life of 20 to 25 days when stored in a cool, dry place. Milk-based sweets like Doodh Pedha are best consumed within 7 to 10 days.

Can we order custom branded boxes for corporate gifting or weddings?

Yes, absolutely! We provide bespoke packaging with your monogram, company branding, and personalized ribbon finishes. We recommend booking wedding orders at least 10–14 days in advance.

Do you deliver outside Hubbali?

We offer same-day delivery across Hubbali-Dharwad. For nearby Karnataka districts and corporate bulk orders, we offer safe temperature-controlled courier packaging.
